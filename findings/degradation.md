# FCB-8: degradation simulator

Code: `src/possession/degrade.py`. Report: `degradation-report.json`, generated by `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python scripts/degrade_report.py` on a clean tree, with the commit, base seed and each match's effective config (including its seed) recorded inside. **Dev matches only**; the holdout was not opened.

## What it does (to a copy of the inputs; labels are never touched)

1. **Ball dropout.**
   - A two-state burst chain decides when the ball goes missing. Its entry probability rises with crowding (players within 3 m) and with ball speed (a proxy for balls in the air).
   - A ball off the pitch is forced missing on 90% of frames, because the camera covers the pitch. The burst chain can add to that, so the final missing share off the pitch can be higher.
   - The base rate is calibrated per match so the ball is visible on **59.3%** of period frames.
2. **Player dropout** in bursts, calibrated so the mean visible object count is **20.5**.
3. **Fragmentation and identity loss.** Each visible run of a player, plus random breaks (0.5 per minute), becomes a tracklet. Tracklets, ghosts, speed derivatives and burst chains are all cut or reset at period boundaries (observable metadata). Tracklets are placed in a free slot of a fixed **46-slot pool** (46 is published; the even 23/23 team split is an assumption), chosen at random, so identities are not preserved, as in the ATD.
4. **Occasional ghost duplicates**, offset by about 1 m, giving more than 22 objects on about 1% of frames.
5. **Jitter:** 0.15 m on players and 0.30 m on the ball.

**Calibration targets are DATA.md published input statistics only:**
- ball visible on 59.3% of graded frames;
- mean 20.5 tracked objects;
- 46 slots;
- "occasionally" more than 22 objects.

Gap lengths, crowding and speed weights, break rate, ghost rate and jitter are **unpublished, so they are fixed assumptions** in `Config`. They are not fitted to target data or to any score.

## Achieved on the 5 dev matches

| statistic | target | achieved |
|---|---|---|
| ball visible (period frames) | 59.3% | 58.6–59.6% |
| mean visible objects | 20.5 | 20.45–20.53 |
| frames with more than 22 objects | "occasionally" | 0.3–1.1% |
| slots | 46 | 46 |
| tracklets lost because the pool was full | — | 0 |

Bias check, ball on the pitch only:
- ball missing when ≥ 3 players are within 3 m: 28–63%;
- ball missing when nobody is within 3 m: 13–29%.

An earlier version of this check, which included off-pitch frames, ran backwards because the forced off-pitch loss confounded it. That was fixed before this report.

## Limitations
- **Burst lengths and how loss depends on game state are assumed.** The target's true gap-length distribution is not published, so FCB-9 should report results under the default plus at least one alternative `Config` (for example shorter and longer gaps) as a robustness band, not a selection target.
- **Crowding is measured on clean positions.** Real ATD loss also depends on camera occlusion geometry, which is not modelled.
- **Same-team identity swaps** are implicit in random re-slotting. Cross-team confusion is not simulated, because DATA.md gives team membership per slot.
